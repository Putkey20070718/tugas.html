How to Brew Coffee

A simple website that contains an easy way to make a cup of coffee.

Tools and Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons of ground coffee
  • 200 ml of hot water
  • Sugar to taste
  • 1 glass
  • 1 spoon

Steps

  1. Prepare a glass and a spoon.
  2. Put 2 tablespoons of ground coffee into the glass.
  3. Add sugar according to your taste.
  4. Pour hot water into the glass.
  5. Stir the coffee until well mixed.
  6. Wait a few minutes so the coffee is not too hot.
  7. The coffee is ready to enjoy.

Coffee Brewing Tips

Use enough hot water and adjust the amount of coffee and sugar according to your personal taste.
